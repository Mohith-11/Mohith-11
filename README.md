## 🧭 About Me

I'm **Mohith V**, an AI & ML undergrad pursuing a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Chennai Institute of Technology *(CGPA 8.82)*. I build applied AI systems that turn raw data (video, audio, text, network traffic) into decisions people can act on.

- 🎥 **Computer vision in production style:** multi-camera analytics with YOLO11m, BoT-SORT, NVIDIA DeepStream and TensorRT (Python + C++).
- 🧠 **RAG & evaluation:** citation-backed retrieval pipelines, and benchmarking 6 medical LLMs on 200 questions across 15+ metrics.
- 🎙️ **Speech & NLP:** combining speech emotion recognition, ASR and text analysis to judge context, not just keywords.
- 🛡️ **ML for security:** DDoS detection on CICIDS2017 with **99.73%** average 5-fold CV F1.
- 🛠️ **Backend by default:** FastAPI, Flask, WebSockets, Redis, Celery, SQLite.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🍔 [QSR Analytics](https://github.com/techytales/QSR_Project_Stud) — Multi-camera restaurant video analytics

> Turns camera footage into numbers managers can use: occupancy, queues, dwell time and staff/customer movement.

- **Highlights:** 11 analytics modules including cross-camera identity, zone analytics and heatmaps, with an end-to-end pipeline and web dashboard.
- **Stack:** `Python` · `C++` · `YOLO11m` · `BoT-SORT` · `NVIDIA DeepStream` · `TensorRT` · `SQLite`

---

### 🏥 [MediCare AI](https://github.com/ggokuldas06/triage_kanini) — AI-based patient triage

> A real-time triage system built at Pragyan × Kanini **(2nd place, 1,300+ teams)**.

- **Features:** 4-level risk prediction with priority/confidence scoring and explainable factors, live queue updates over WebSockets, OCR prescription scanning and LLM-based symptom extraction.
- **Stack:** `Random Forest` · `WebSockets` · `OCR` · `LLMs`

---

### 🎙️ [CallShield-AI](https://github.com/Mohith-11/Callshield-AI) — Call threat detection using SER and NLP

> A real-time pipeline that judges high-risk calls in context by combining tone, transcript and text, instead of relying on keywords alone.

- **Features:** audio preprocessing, speech emotion recognition, ASR, threat-level scoring and interactive visualizations.
- **Stack:** `Python` · `FastAPI` · `SER` · `ASR` · `NLP`

---

### 🩺 [Med-RAG](https://github.com/Mohith-11/Med-RAG) — Medical Q&A with retrieval-augmented generation

> Built during my AI internship at NIT Puducherry.

- **Pipeline:** 5-pass retrieval (preprocessing, embeddings, semantic vector search, LLM generation) for evidence-based, citation-backed answers.
- **Evaluation:** benchmarked 6 medical LLMs on 200 questions with 15+ metrics *(BERTScore F1 0.8915, Faithfulness 0.8710, MRR 0.8308)*.
- **Stack:** `Python` · `FastAPI` · `Embeddings` · `Vector Search`

---

### 🛡️ [DDoS Detection and Prevention](https://github.com/Mohith-11/DDOS-detection-and-prevention) — Adaptive ML model

> Built during my ML internship at the Cybersecurity Lab (Centre of Excellence).

- **Highlights:** cleaned ~1M CICIDS2017 records (100,463 duplicates removed), **99.73%** average 5-fold CV F1, real-time flow monitoring and alerts with adaptive retraining.
- **Stack:** `Random Forest` · `SMOTE` · `RobustScaler` · `VarianceThreshold`
